Service FAQs

Common questions about our programs and admissions.

Do you offer half-day care programs?

Yes, we offer highly flexible scheduling. Families can choose from full-day, half-day, or specific hourly drop-ins depending on availability and care needs.

Is medical staff always on site?

Absolutely. A registered nurse is present during all operating hours to handle medication administration, vital monitoring, and emergencies.

Are meals included in the daily rate?

Yes, a full-day admission includes a morning snack, a hot lunch, and an afternoon tea service tailored to dietary restrictions.

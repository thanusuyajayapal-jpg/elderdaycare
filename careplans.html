Membership Questions

Common questions regarding our billing, plans, and admissions process.

Can I change my plan later?
Yes, absolutely. We operate on a month-to-month basis without locking you into rigid long-term contracts. If your loved one's needs increase or decrease, simply notify our billing department by the 15th of the month to adjust your tier for the following cycle.
Is financial assistance or insurance accepted?
We accept several major long-term care insurance policies as well as Medicaid (for eligible participants). We also partner with local state programs to provide subsidized care tiers for lower-income families. Please call our admissions office for a detailed review of your benefits.
What happens if my loved one misses a day?
For monthly plan members, we unfortunately cannot refund individual missed days due to illness or personal reasons. However, if a prolonged absence (over 5 consecutive days) occurs due to hospitalization, we can pause billing or apply a prorated credit to the next month's invoice.
